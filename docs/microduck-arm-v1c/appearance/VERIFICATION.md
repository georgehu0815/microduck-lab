## Not verified

- Added physical shells: mass, inertia, full-range clearance, fasteners, seams, thermal
  behavior, harness fit, impact strength, manufacturability and hardware behavior.
- OpenSCAD compilation: the executable is not installed; exported source is a review
  envelope study rather than a compiled solid or fabrication package.
- New task policy training, physical robot operation, new payload capability or a
  release-height improvement. Training smoke tests in the regression suite are not
  task-policy qualification.
- Every historical standalone-arm task under the new render wrapper: the motion evidence
  here is specifically the preserved v13 tennis matrix, not a newly executed universal
  task matrix. Other relevant code paths are covered by the regression selection.

The interrupted first-look replay is preserved in `interrupted-draft-replays/` and is
not published as successful evidence.
